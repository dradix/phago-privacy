Privacy Policy

Phago: Immune Defense (package com.jviveros.phago) · Effective October 18, 2026

Phago: Immune Defense ("the game") is developed by Jesus Viveros (Viveros Corp). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not run its own servers.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, best waves, memory cells, upgrades, vaccines, settings and an in-progress run) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The game offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks all levels. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; the developer never receives your payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play and stores a flag on your device that the content is unlocked. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it to unlock achievements and post scores to leaderboards (total waves survived and total memory cells earned). Google Play Games may show your Play Games profile name and avatar alongside these. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in, and you can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.

Crash and performance reports

Google Play may collect anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals) according to your device settings. The developer can only see aggregated, non-identifying information from these reports.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No collection of contacts, location, photos, microphone or other personal data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
